# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

- First release: a jar holding one marble for every Saturday until a child turns eighteen; each week's marble fills with colour by the time spent together, then goes into the tray.
- The honest mirror: phone minutes beside time with the children in the family hours, evening by evening, with the apps that took the evenings; the optional shield.
- Time together with the phone put away; the minutes it was picked up are left out, measured by the phone's own usage history.
- Ideas by how much battery is left, five-minute everyday rituals, bedtime questions, what they're into now, one on one, plans and a weekly note about the age.
- The month's letter and the almanac as a printable PDF; pictures with the phone's own camera; backup to one zip. English and Russian.
